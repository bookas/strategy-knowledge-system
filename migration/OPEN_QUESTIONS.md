v# Strategy Knowledge System — Open Questions Register

## 1. Document Purpose

This document records unresolved questions that remain materially relevant to the Strategy Knowledge System at the time of migration.

Its purpose is to preserve uncertainty deliberately.

An open question is not automatically a defect.

A question may remain open because:

- evidence is insufficient;
- credible theories disagree;
- the issue belongs to a later research block;
- a boundary remains unresolved;
- the question depends on organizational evidence;
- architecture review is still required;
- the issue has been intentionally deferred.

This document is not:

- a task list;
- a migration checklist;
- a backlog of minor improvements;
- a place to repeat all known research findings;
- a substitute for the Conceptual Debt Register that may later be created.

During migration, it acts as the consolidated register of important unresolved questions.

---

# 2. Status Vocabulary

Each question may use one of the following statuses:

**Open**  
Requires future investigation.

**Deferred**  
Known issue intentionally postponed.

**Research Scheduled**  
Expected to be addressed in a defined future research block.

**Architecture Review Required**  
Cannot be resolved responsibly within a single chapter.

**Evidence Required**  
Resolution depends primarily on additional evidence.

**Application Dependent**  
Cannot be answered universally without organizational context.

**Under Review**  
Currently being actively reconsidered.

**Resolved**  
Question has been sufficiently answered.

**Superseded**  
Question was reframed or replaced by another question.

Resolved or superseded questions should normally remain in the register for provenance.

---

# 3. Priority Vocabulary

Where useful:

**P1 — System-Forming**  
Potentially affects foundational concepts or large parts of the Knowledge System.

**P2 — Significant**  
Important but manageable without blocking most downstream work.

**P3 — Local / Deferred**  
Useful future clarification with limited immediate downstream risk.

Priority does not determine truth or research sequence automatically.

# OQ-001 — What Is the Minimum Definition of Strategy?

**Priority:** P1  
**Status:** Open  
**Origin:** Part 01, especially RQ-01, RQ-05, RQ-10, RQ-11

## Question

What is the thinnest definition of Strategy that can include both deliberate/formulated and realized/emergent Strategy without becoming so broad that ordinary organizational behavior also qualifies as Strategy?

## Why It Remains Open

Part 01 progressively weakened several candidate universal requirements:

- explicit prior choice;
- explicit diagnosis;
- explicit causal logic;
- formal commitment architecture.

Those remain strong quality requirements for deliberate Strategy but cannot safely define all realized Strategy.

The final minimum definition therefore needs to be thinner than the full deliberate-strategy quality checklist.

## Current Working Position

Strategy appears to require some strategically significant pattern, logic, or configuration connecting organizational direction, behavior, and consequential allocation under constraints and uncertainty.

This remains a synthesis candidate, not a final locked definition.

## Dependencies

- Part 01 Controlled Revision
- Consolidated Synthesis
- deliberate/emergent architecture
- strategic agency
- quality/failure findings

## Revisit

Before Part 01 v1.0 release.

# OQ-002 — What Constitutes Strategic Agency?

**Priority:** P1  
**Status:** Open  
**Origin:** RQ-03, REV-002, RQ-10

## Question

Who or what must possess strategic agency for Strategy to exist?

Can Strategy form without a clearly identifiable prior strategic chooser?

## Why It Remains Open

Deliberate Strategy usually implies identifiable decision authority.

Emergent Strategy may instead arise through:

- distributed action;
- repeated local choices;
- organizational routines;
- retrospective recognition;
- selection;
- legitimation.

Formation, articulation, selection, and authorization may belong to different actors.

## Current Working Position

Level, object, actor, agency, and decision authority must remain distinct.

A prior identifiable chooser cannot currently be treated as a universal requirement.

## Dependencies

- organizational theory
- emergence
- power/politics
- governance
- future Strategy Formulation research

## Revisit

During Part 01 synthesis and later Strategy Formulation research.

# 6. Open Question Governance

When future research addresses an Open Question:

1. Do not delete the original entry.
2. Update its status.
3. Link the research artifact that addressed it.
4. Record whether the answer:
   - resolved;
   - narrowed;
   - reframed;
   - split;
   - superseded
   the question.
5. Create new Open Questions if the research exposes materially different uncertainties.

An Open Question may become more precise rather than disappear.
